Manifest proof application

This app exists to prove one thing: it knows who signed in, and one person cannot see another person's notes.

  • Sign in with CWL
  • /api/me — what the IdP released about you
  • /api/notes — your notes, and nobody else's
  • /api/ai — 501 until P4b
  • Sign out

A note is stored under sha256(puid ‖ project ‖ environment) and never under a UBC identifier, so this database holds nothing that identifies a person on its own.

make demo-identity drives all of it with curl.
