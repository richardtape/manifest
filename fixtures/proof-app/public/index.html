Manifest proof application

This app exists to prove three things: it knows who signed in, one person cannot see another person's notes, and a question is answered from your notes and charged to you.

  • Sign in with CWL
  • /api/me — what the IdP released about you
  • /api/notes — your notes, and nobody else's
  • POST /api/ask — a question, answered with one of your own notes as context
  • Sign out

A note is stored under sha256(puid ‖ project ‖ environment) and never under a UBC identifier, so this database holds nothing that identifies a person on its own. The same string is what the AI service charges a question to, so one application's spending can never lock you out of another's.

make demo-identity drives the sign-in and the notes with curl; make demo-ai drives the question.
