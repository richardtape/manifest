Why these are kept apart

One conversation is one piece of work. Keeping them separate is what lets you abandon one without losing another — and lets you see which one caused a change six weeks later.

Several can run at once. Only one can be putting something live at a time.

Nothing is lost when one ends

A conversation that stops leaves your app exactly as it found it. Whatever it did put on an address stays there until something replaces it.
